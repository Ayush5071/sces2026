Instructions for Authors

Instructions for Authors:

  • First author of each submitted paper must be a student.
  • Prospective authors are invited to submit original and unpublished papers to the conference.
  • Papers should be submitted through the online paper submission system, using the prescribed paper template, available on this page (see Paper Templates below).
  • All submitted papers will be screened for plagiarism. Papers found to violate the conference's publication and ethical guidelines may be rejected.
  • All eligible submissions will undergo a peer-review process, and the acceptance decision will be based on the recommendations of the reviewers and the Technical Program Committee.
  • At least one author of each accepted paper must present the paper at the conference.
  • Only accepted and duly presented papers that meet the conference's publication requirements will be considered for inclusion in IEEE Xplore®.

Best Paper Awards:

  • A Best Paper Award will be given in each technical track.
