View Call for Special Session Proposals

Submission Guidelines

  • Papers submitted to a Special Session follow the same format and page limit as regular SCES-2027 papers — a maximum of 6 pages including references.
  • All Special Session papers undergo the standard peer-review process, with each paper reviewed by a minimum of three reviewers.
  • Submissions are made through the SCES-2027 Microsoft CMT portal, selecting the relevant Special Session track.
  • Please refer to the Call for Papers and the guidelines for paper submission for templates and formatting requirements.

For queries regarding Special Sessions, write to sces@mnnit.ac.in.
